How much does a website cost?

A standard 5-page website starts from KES 45,000. Larger builds are scoped with fixed pricing before work begins.

How much does CCTV installation cost?

CCTV starts from KES 25,000 for a basic 4-camera HD setup. We provide a free site survey and itemised quotation for every project.

Do you serve clients outside Nairobi?

Yes — Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, and internationally in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and Ethiopia.

Do you accept M-Pesa?

Yes — M-Pesa, bank transfer, and staged payment plans. Typically 50% deposit on signing and 50% on completion.

What warranty do ICT installations carry?

12-month workmanship warranty plus full manufacturer warranty (1–3 years). Annual SLA contracts available.

How long do projects take?

A standard website: 2–4 weeks. CCTV or access control: 2–5 working days. Larger projects delivered in phases with timelines provided upfront.
