FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before picking up the phone — though we're always happy to answer on WhatsApp too.

How much does CCTV installation cost in Nairobi?

CCTV installation in Nairobi starts from KES 25,000 for a basic 4-camera HD setup and goes up to KES 150,000+ for a full IP-based enterprise system with night vision, remote mobile access, and motion analytics. Amari Digital provides free site surveys and itemised quotations with zero obligation — so you know exactly what you're paying for before we start.

Do you serve clients outside Nairobi?

Absolutely. We have active projects across Kenya — Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, and Kitengela — and we operate internationally in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and Ethiopia. Travel and logistics costs are included transparently in every proposal — no surprise invoices.

What CCTV and security brands do you install?

We are authorised installers and partners for Hikvision, Dahua, Axis, and Bosch — the most widely used and best-supported CCTV brands in Kenya. For networks we work with Cisco, Huawei, and Ubiquiti. For AV, we use Samsung, LG, Crestron, and Logitech. We recommend based on your budget and requirements — not our margin.

Do you accept M-Pesa?

Yes. We accept M-Pesa, bank transfers (Equity, KCB, NCBA, Co-op), and offer flexible payment plans — typically 50% deposit on signing and 50% on project completion. For larger enterprise projects, we can discuss phased payment structures.

What warranty and after-sales support do you offer?

Every installation comes with a 12-month workmanship warranty covering our labour and configuration, plus the full manufacturer's product warranty (typically 1–3 years depending on brand). We also offer annual SLA maintenance contracts with scheduled preventive maintenance visits, priority response times, and remote monitoring.

How long does a typical installation take?

A standard office CCTV or access control project (up to 20 cameras or 10 doors) typically takes 2–5 working days. Larger projects — hotel IPTV rollouts, full building automation, or campus networking — are scoped in phases with a detailed Gantt chart so you always know what's happening and when.
