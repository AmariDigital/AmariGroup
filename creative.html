  • Legal obligation: Where required to comply with Kenyan law

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by Kenyan law. Enquiry data is retained for a maximum of 3 years. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 9).

9. Your Rights Under the Data Protection Act, 2019

Under Sections 26–35 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the following rights:

  • Right to be informed — to know how your data is used
  • Right of access — to request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification — to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your data
  • Right to restrict processing — to limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at amaridigital@outlook.com. We will respond within 21 days as required by law.

10. Cookies Policy

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyse website traffic. Cookies used include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics — help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Marketing cookies: Meta Pixel — used to measure ad performance

You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though this may affect website functionality.

11. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or destruction, in line with Section 41 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018.

12. Complaints

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC):

  • Address: Teleposta Towers, Nairobi, Kenya
