FAQ

Common
Questions

Everything you need to know before reaching out — though we're always happy to chat on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does a website cost?
A standard 5-page business website starts from KES 45,000. Larger custom builds and e-commerce platforms are scoped individually with fixed pricing before work begins.
How much does CCTV installation cost?
CCTV starts from KES 25,000 for a basic 4-camera HD setup. We provide a free site survey and itemised quotation for every project.
Do you serve clients outside Nairobi?
Yes — Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, and internationally across Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and Ethiopia. Travel costs are included transparently in every proposal.
Do you accept M-Pesa?
Yes. M-Pesa, bank transfer (Equity, KCB, NCBA, Co-op), and staged payment plans — typically 50% deposit on signing and 50% on completion.
What warranty do ICT installations carry?
All installations include a 12-month workmanship warranty plus the full manufacturer warranty (1–3 years depending on brand). Annual SLA maintenance contracts are available.
How long do projects typically take?
A standard website takes 2–4 weeks. A basic CCTV or access control project takes 2–5 working days. Larger projects are delivered in phases with a detailed timeline provided upfront.
